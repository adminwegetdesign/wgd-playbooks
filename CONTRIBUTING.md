# Contributing

Thanks for looking. A few plain rules so expectations are clear.

## What we welcome

- **Issues** reporting something broken, out of date or unclear.
- **Pointer suggestions:** a resource you have used, with a line on why and when.
- **Corrections** to typos and dead links, by pull request.

## What we can't do

- We don't offer support or set-up help through this repo.
- We don't accept new playbooks from outside WGD for now. Every kit here is something we use ourselves, and we want to keep it that way.

## Before you open an issue

- Check the playbook's "What it doesn't do" section.
- Never post client data, credentials or internal URLs from your own organisation.

## Review

Every change lands by pull request and is reviewed by WGD before merging.
