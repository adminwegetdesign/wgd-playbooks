# Know

Is your business knowledge written down, owned and current?

AI is only as reliable as what it reads. Playbooks here help you decide what is authoritative, give every record an owner and a review date, and mark what has been replaced.

## Playbooks

| Playbook | What it gives you | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Knowledge-base starter](knowledge-base-starter/) | A plain-text knowledge base for one process, with rules an AI assistant can follow and a ten-question test | About 45 minutes |
