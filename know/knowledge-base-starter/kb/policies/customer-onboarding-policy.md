# Customer onboarding policy

*Fictional example. Delete before use.*

## Purpose

Sets what must happen before work starts for a new customer.

## Content

1. A signed agreement is on file before any work starts.
2. The account manager runs a kick-off call within five working days of signature.
3. The customer names one decision-maker and one day-to-day contact.
4. The first invoice is raised on signature, not at kick-off.

## Exceptions

Existing customers starting a new project skip step 3 unless their contacts have changed. Anything else goes to the Head of Operations.
