# Monthly client report procedure

*Fictional example. Delete before use.*

## Purpose

How the monthly report for each active client is prepared and approved.

## Content

1. **Pull this month's completed and open tasks** from the project management system. Done when: you have both lists.
2. **Write a three-line summary in plain English** at the top: what was done, what's next, anything the client must decide. Done when: a non-technical reader understands it without the rest.
3. **List risks and blockers** with an owner for each. Done when: no risk is unowned.
4. **Remove internal notes,** costs and other clients' names. Done when: a second person has checked.
5. **Send to the Delivery Lead for approval** before it goes to the client. Done when: approval is recorded.

## Exceptions

Clients on a fixed-price project with no activity that month get a one-line note instead.
