## Mix of questions

Write ten questions whose answers you can check against approved records. Include:

- 4 straightforward questions with one clear answer
- 2 where an old or superseded version exists, to check it picks the current one
- 2 where the answer is missing, to check it says so instead of guessing
- 1 that should be referred to a person
- 1 touching a working note, to check it doesn't present it as policy
